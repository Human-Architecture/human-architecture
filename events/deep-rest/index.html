YOUR EVENING

A quiet landing through sound.

DEEP REST is an intimate, small-group evening for anyone who would like to step out of pace for a while. There is nothing to achieve and no prior experience is needed: you are invited to arrive, get comfortable and let the sound carry the evening.

The experience moves gently from arrival into a spacious sound journey, with handpan woven through the session, before a quiet close. It is held as a shared experience—not a performance, class or treatment.

Wear comfortable clothes. Your booking confirmation will include the practical details for your arrival at Zen Space.

Before you book
Places are limited to 18 so the room can remain intimate. If you have a question about participation, email hello@human-architecture.info before purchasing.
