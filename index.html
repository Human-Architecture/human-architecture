02

Orientation before intervention

Every human system already has a structure.

Not everyone has learned how to read it.

Human Architecture® is an integrative framework for understanding how a human system is currently organized.

It considers the relationships between blueprint, body structure, movement, breathing, regulation, recovery, identity and integration.

Rather than beginning with a predetermined method, the work begins by identifying the most relevant domain and the structurally appropriate point of entry.

Alignment with inherent structure — not generalized self-improvement.
