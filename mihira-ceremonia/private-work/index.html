Private work / One to one

For what cannot
be met at the surface.

Private work for people in consequential transitions who know they cannot enter what comes next as the person they had to become.

01 / The deeper proposition

The visible question is where we enter.
It is rarely where we remain.

A decision may be surrounded by identity, belonging, loyalty, fear and grief. A business question may be carrying power, worth, protection and permission. A relationship pattern may be more intelligent—and more costly—than it first appears.

Private work gives the context, attention and relational depth required to see what public guidance cannot responsibly decide for you.

03 / Fit

This work is for depth, not dependency.

A strong fit

A real threshold or consequential choice is present.

The visible problem no longer explains the whole situation.

You value nuance, honest challenge and personal responsibility.

You are ready to translate insight into lived choices.

Not the right fit

You want Mihira to prescribe an identity or decision.

You are seeking reassurance without examination.

You expect guaranteed outcomes or instant certainty.

You need clinical, legal, financial or crisis support outside scope.
