01 / Mihira Ceremonia

Not another method for becoming more acceptable.

Mihira is a private practice for people whose outer lives can no longer be understood separately from the human architecture underneath them.

We begin with the human—not to center the self above consequence, but to understand what is actually shaping the choice, relationship, leadership or life being lived.

The work is intimate, exact and contextual. It does not prescribe an identity. It makes the architecture visible enough for a more authored relationship with what comes next.

Mel / Founder

The person who sees what others miss.

Mel works at the meeting point of pattern, relationship, consequence and truth. Mihira Ceremonia is the house that holds that thinking—and the people ready to encounter themselves without reduction.
