WHAT THIS IS FOR

Not another return to square one.

Body Fahrplan does not look for blame or discipline. It creates a clear snapshot: what supports your everyday life, where friction arises, and which next step is truly small enough to live.

01

See the present rhythm

We map daily rhythm, demands, time, rest, access and social context—without turning them into a premature explanation.

02

Place recurring friction in context

We look at previous restarts: what supported you for a while, what became too tight, and what changed in the life around it?

03

Choose a realistic next framework

You leave with a few concrete orientation points, a boundary against overload, and useful questions for further qualified support.

AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

The body is not a character test.

Weight, energy, eating patterns and movement are affected by many conditions. This Codex does not replace medical, dietetic or psychological assessment, and makes no claim to explain why a body has a particular weight.

It is not a weight-loss programme, nutrition or training prescription, diagnosis, or a promise to lose or maintain weight.

HOW IT BEGINS

A clear frame before anything begins.

  1. 01Send a brief enquiry

    In a few sentences, describe what keeps repeating and what you would like to change right now.

  2. 02Clarify fit and boundaries

    You receive a clear reply about whether this format fits—or whether other qualified support makes more sense first.

  3. 03Confirm scope in writing

    Focus, process, price and the exact form of support are agreed transparently before any commitment.

WHEN OTHER SUPPORT SHOULD COME FIRST

Health deserves the right setting.

If weight, eating, mood or physical symptoms are worrying you—or if you are seeking medical, dietetic or psychological care—a doctor, qualified dietitian or mental-health professional is the right first step. Body Fahrplan cannot replace that work.

CONFIDENTIAL ENQUIRY

You do not need to explain your life perfectly first.

A few honest sentences are enough to clarify fit. There is no automatic booking and no promise—just a clear, respectful next step.
