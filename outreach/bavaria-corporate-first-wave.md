# Bavaria corporate outreach — first wave

Purpose: open six considered conversations with hospitality, offsite and business-event partners in Bavaria. The first contact is an invitation to assess fit, not a promise of a programme or an outcome.

## The offer, kept simple

Lead with one of three experiences:

1. **BODY LANGUAGE** — guided movement and body-awareness workshop; participants leave with three personal focus areas for their own practice.
2. **RETURN** — a deliberately held pause using breathwork, body awareness and Yoga Nidra.
3. **DEEP REST** — Yoga Nidra with optional live handpan, for arrival, a transition or a close.

Yoga or guided movement can be included when it serves the group and venue; it is not presented as a fourth standalone offer. Before any confirmation, agree purpose, group size, setting, accessibility, duration, travel, fee and booking route in writing.

## Sending sequence

- Send three messages in week one and three in week two, Tuesday to Thursday between 09:30 and 11:00 local time.
- Do not attach a deck to the first message. Link to the corporate page only if requested: `https://www.human-architecture.info/organizations/international/`.
- Send one short follow-up after seven working days. If there is no response, leave the relationship open and do not chase.
- Use a named recipient only when a current contact person is confirmed; otherwise address the events or meetings team.
