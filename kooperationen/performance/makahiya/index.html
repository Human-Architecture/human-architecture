THE FIT

Personal orientation within Makahiya’s training experience.

Makahiya already offers HYROX, strength, yoga, mobility, coaching and recovery at its Unawatuna fitness hotel. The useful opening is more specific: help each guest notice what deserves attention in their own movement, then bring one or two practical cues back into training with Makahiya’s coaches.

The proposed classes are low-load, exploratory sessions. They complement the existing timetable and coaching; they are not replacement yoga, a new training plan or a promise to prevent injury.

Based on Makahiya’s public classes, facilities and recovery offering, and guest package. Current timetable and guest needs to be confirmed directly with the team.

WHAT GUESTS ACTUALLY GAIN

One observation. One usable next step.

01 / NOTICE

Explore a pattern

Simple movement options make it easier to observe range, control, effort and breathing without ranking guests against one another.

02 / CHOOSE

Name a focus

Guests leave each class with one personal point of attention and a brief practice option suitable for their current capacity.

03 / CONNECT

Bring it to training

With the guest’s consent, the focus can become a useful question for Makahiya’s coach. The coach retains training decisions and progression.

A THREE-CLASS WEEKLY PILOT

One rhythm. Three useful entry points.

Example: one class in each category per week for an initial four-week pilot. Final days and times follow the centre’s timetable and training load.

01 / HIPS & LOWER BODY

Hip Mobility & Control

45–60 minutes. Yoga-informed movement with active hip rotation, extension and flexion, plus pelvic and ankle options. Guests compare comfortable range with controlled movement relevant to running, lunges and squat patterns. No forced end-range stretching.

Leave with: one individual lower-body focus and a short practice option.

02 / UPPER BODY

Shoulders & Thoracic Mobility

45–60 minutes. Scapular movement, thoracic rotation, overhead range and controlled shoulder loading. Relevant to SkiErg, rowing, carries and wall balls. Range is balanced with control; discomfort is not pushed through.

Leave with: one shoulder or upper-back focus to discuss with a coach.

03 / BREATH & PACING

Breathwork for Training & Downshift

35–45 minutes. Comfortable breathing awareness, noticing effort and easing into a post-training downshift. This is distinct from Makahiya’s supervised pool or breath-hold activities: no maximal holds, hyperventilation or performance claims. Every exercise is optional.

Leave with: one usable breathing cue for a chosen context.

These are programming hypotheses, not a claim that all HYROX athletes have tight hips, restricted shoulders or dysfunctional breathing. Coaches keep responsibility for training plans, technique and progression. New pain, injury or medical concerns need appropriate clinical advice.

Three classes mean three sessions total each week, not three per day. Timing and intensity should be checked against Makahiya’s live timetable, guest turnover, heat and coach priorities.

SEPARATE SIGNATURE WORKSHOP

BODY LANGUAGE, monthly to start.

A 90–120 minute practical workshop where each participant explores mobility, stability, strength, movement patterns, breath and recovery, then identifies three personal training focuses. Offer once a month as a pilot; consider twice monthly only if demand, space and facilitator capacity justify it.

For a short-stay guest this can create orientation early in the visit. The weekly classes let them explore a focus in more depth. Makahiya’s coaches remain responsible for individual training plans and progression. BODY LANGUAGE stays a distinct bookable workshop.

A SMALL TEST BEFORE THREE MONTHS

Four weeks first. Expand on evidence.

  1. Meet the programming lead and review the actual class calendar, guest turnover and coach handoff.
  2. Agree a four-week pilot: up to 12 weekly classes and one BODY LANGUAGE workshop, with exact times and capacity set together.
  3. Review attendance, guest feedback and whether coaches find the focus handoff useful. Feedback is voluntary and shared only as agreed.
  4. Decide whether to keep, adjust or stop the format before any extension through March.

January–March 2027 is an exploratory delivery window, not a booked three-month engagement. The first four weeks establish whether the programme adds enough value for Makahiya’s guests and team.

No health outcome, injury prevention, race-time improvement or class attendance is guaranteed.

ROLES & TERMS

Clear before anything is announced.

Makahiya: timetable input, suitable space, guest/member registration and communications, agreed mats or equipment, on-site coordination and coaching interface.

Human Architecture: agreed class design, preparation, facilitation, accessible variations, BODY LANGUAGE delivery and a short pilot review.

The pilot can be host-funded, offered as a paid guest add-on, or combine a base fee with ticket income. The chosen model must clarify inclusions for each Makahiya stay package, minimum numbers, booking ownership and settlement.

Programme

Confirm duration, exact slots, group size, audience, language, space and any coach coordination.

Commercial terms

Agree fee, currency, guest price if any, travel/accommodation, cancellations and payment schedule.

Delivery

Confirm insurance, local work feasibility, safeguarding, consent and who handles any medical concerns. Shared promotion and use of Makahiya’s brand require approval.
