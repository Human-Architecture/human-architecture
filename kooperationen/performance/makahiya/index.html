Train hard. Move with more understanding.

THE FIT

A thoughtful layer within a strong training environment.

Makahiya already offers HYROX, strength, mobility, yoga and recovery. This concept proposes a specific, repeatable complement: space to observe how the hips, shoulders and breath work during training, with options guests can carry into their coached sessions.

It does not replace existing classes or claim to correct every athlete. Class content and placement would be developed with Makahiya’s team after reviewing its actual timetable and guest mix.

A THREE-CLASS WEEKLY PILOT

One rhythm. Three useful entry points.

Example: one class in each category per week for an initial four-week pilot. Final days and times follow the centre’s timetable and training load.

01 / HIPS & LOWER BODY

Hip Mobility & Control

45–60 minutes. Controlled hip rotation, extension and flexion; ankle and pelvic options; active range and load-aware movement. Relevant to running, lunges and squat-based work. A yoga-informed class with options rather than long, forced stretches.

Leave with: one individual lower-body movement focus and a short practice option.

02 / UPPER BODY

Shoulders & Thoracic Mobility

45–60 minutes. Scapular movement, thoracic rotation, overhead range and controlled shoulder loading. Relevant to SkiErg, rowing, carries and wall balls. Range is balanced with control; discomfort is not pushed through.

Leave with: one shoulder or upper-back focus to discuss with a coach.

03 / BREATH & PACING

Breathwork for Training & Downshift

35–45 minutes. Comfortable breathing awareness, pacing between efforts and a gentle post-training downshift. No maximal breath holds, hyperventilation protocols or performance promises; guests may opt out of any exercise.

Leave with: one practical breathing cue for a chosen context.

These are programming hypotheses, not a claim that all HYROX athletes have tight hips, restricted shoulders or dysfunctional breathing. Coaches keep responsibility for training plans, technique and progression. New pain, injury or medical concerns need appropriate clinical advice.

Three classes mean three sessions total each week, not three per day. Timing and intensity should be checked against Makahiya’s live timetable, guest turnover, heat and coach priorities.

SEPARATE SIGNATURE WORKSHOP

BODY LANGUAGE, monthly to start.

A 90–120 minute practical workshop where each participant explores movement, stability, strength, breath and recovery, then identifies three personal training focuses. Offer once a month as a pilot; consider twice monthly only if demand, space and facilitator capacity justify it.

This is a distinct bookable workshop, not a substitute for the weekly classes. It can give new guests an orientation and returning guests a fresh look at priorities.

A SMALL TEST BEFORE THREE MONTHS

Four weeks first. Expand on evidence.

  1. Confirm audience, timetable gaps, room and equipment.
  2. Agree a four-week pilot: 12 weekly classes plus one BODY LANGUAGE workshop, subject to start date.
  3. Gather simple, voluntary feedback on usefulness, attendance and fit with existing programming.
  4. Decide together whether and how to continue through March.

January–March 2027 is an exploratory delivery window, not a booked three-month engagement. A staged agreement keeps the programme useful for short-stay guests while giving Makahiya a genuine choice after the pilot.

No health outcome, injury prevention, race-time improvement or class attendance is guaranteed.

ROLES & TERMS

Clear before anything is announced.

Makahiya: timetable input, suitable space, guest/member registration and communications, agreed mats or equipment, on-site coordination and coaching interface.

Human Architecture: agreed class design, preparation, facilitation, accessible variations, BODY LANGUAGE delivery and a short pilot review.

We would agree exact dates, capacity, language, booking and cancellation flow, fee structure, currency, travel/accommodation, liability and destination-specific work feasibility in writing. Any use of Makahiya’s name or brand in shared promotion requires their approval.
