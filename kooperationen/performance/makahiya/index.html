THE STRATEGIC FIT

Not more programming. A clearer thread through the stay.

Makahiya already connects accommodation, daily classes, personal coaching, nutritious meals and recovery. Human Architecture can sit between those parts as an orientation layer: helping a guest notice a physical pattern, choose one priority and—only with their consent—take a concise, useful question back into coaching.

The proposed sessions are low-load and exploratory. They do not replace Makahiya’s programming, prescribe a training plan or promise injury prevention. Makahiya’s coaches retain responsibility for technique, progression and training decisions.

Public-fit review updated 6 October 2026 using Makahiya’s official About, Classes, Packages and Recovery pages. The live timetable, guest turnover, current priorities and operational fit still require direct confirmation.

VALUE AT THREE LEVELS

Useful to the guest. Clear for the coach. Coherent for the stay.

01 / GUEST

Personal relevance

A guest leaves with a specific point of attention—not a generic list of mobility drills or another full programme to follow.

02 / COACH

A cleaner handoff

With permission, one concise observation can become a question inside coaching. Makahiya’s coach decides what is useful and what changes, if anything.

03 / MAKAHIYA

A more connected stay

The guest can understand how class, coaching and recovery relate, without adding a competing training track to Makahiya’s ecosystem.

The proposition: guests do not simply complete more sessions. They understand more clearly what they are doing, what they are noticing and where to place attention next.

THE GUEST JOURNEY

From arrival to one usable focus.

01 / ARRIVE

Context

The guest’s goals, training history and current capacity are acknowledged without turning the experience into a medical assessment.

02 / NOTICE

BODY LANGUAGE

A guided workshop helps the guest explore patterns across range, control, strength, breathing and recovery.

03 / EXPLORE

Weekly sessions

Low-load sessions offer optional depth around hips, shoulders, spine, breath and down-regulation.

04 / CONNECT

Coach handoff

If the guest chooses, one concise focus can be carried into Makahiya’s existing personal coaching.

The personal focus belongs to the guest.

Individual observations are not reported to Makahiya automatically. Any handoff is guest-led, concise and agreed in advance.

THE FOUR-WEEK PILOT

One signature workshop. Three complementary weekly sessions.

The workshop creates orientation; the weekly rhythm gives guests selected ways to explore it. Final placement follows Makahiya’s live timetable, training load and guest turnover.

01 / LOWER BODY

Range & Control: Hips

45–60 minutes. Active hip rotation, flexion and extension with pelvic and ankle options. Guests compare available range with controlled movement relevant to running, lunges and squat patterns—without forced end-range stretching.

Guest leaves with: one lower-body focus and a short practice option.

02 / UPPER BODY

Mobility & Control: Shoulders + Spine

45–60 minutes. Scapular movement, thoracic rotation, overhead range and controlled shoulder loading. Relevant to SkiErg, rowing, carries and wall balls without presenting one restriction as universal.

Guest leaves with: one shoulder or upper-back focus to explore.

03 / RECOVERY

Breath & Deep Rest

45–60 minutes. Comfortable breath awareness, a deliberate downshift and guided Yoga Nidra. No maximal holds, hyperventilation, medical claims or pressure to participate at a fixed intensity.

Guest leaves with: one simple cue for returning from effort into rest.

These are programming hypotheses—not a claim that all HYROX athletes share the same hip, shoulder or breathing pattern. Coaches retain responsibility for training plans, technique and progression. New pain, injury or medical concerns require appropriate clinical advice.

FACILITATION · MEL MIHIRA

A complementary layer, not competing coaching.

Mel’s role is to facilitate precise observation: what a guest notices in movement, where control or range deserves attention and which small practice may be useful to explore next.

Her approach brings together a physiotherapy-informed foundation, functional anatomy, structural bodywork, yoga and breath-led practice. It does not diagnose, prescribe a medical treatment or replace Makahiya’s coaches. Training decisions, technique and progression remain with Makahiya’s team.

THE ORIENTATION POINT

BODY LANGUAGE gives the programme its thread.

One 90–120 minute workshop in the initial four weeks helps each participant explore mobility, stability, strength, movement patterns, breath and recovery—then identify three personal training focuses.

Its position should follow Makahiya’s actual guest turnover rather than an arbitrary monthly rhythm. The weekly sessions offer optional depth; Makahiya’s coaches retain responsibility for personal coaching, technique and progression.

THE PILOT DECISION

Four weeks first. Continue only if it earns its place.

  1. Define: review the live timetable, guest turnover, spaces and the exact coach-handoff boundary.
  2. Deliver: one BODY LANGUAGE workshop plus up to three complementary sessions per week.
  3. Learn: track attendance, voluntary guest reflection, coach usefulness and operational friction.
  4. Decide: continue, simplify, reposition or stop before any longer commitment.

January–March 2027 remains an exploratory window—not a booked residency. The first four weeks should establish whether the format adds sufficient value for Makahiya’s guests and team without complicating the existing schedule.

No health outcome, injury prevention, race-time improvement, attendance level or commercial return is guaranteed.

COMMERCIAL ROUTES
