WHY IT FITS

Training has a programme. Every body has a context.

A coached class can challenge and develop a group. A short reflective format helps each person notice where mobility, stability, control, breath, tension or recovery deserves attention within that training.

Human Architecture adds this moment of observation and integration alongside your coaches. Technical instruction, individual programming and progression remain with your team. We agree the guest experience and boundaries before a session is announced.

COMMERCIAL CLARITY

Agree the model before we announce it.

Your centre may fund the workshop, offer paid places, or propose another suitable arrangement. We agree the workshop fee, participant number, duration, currency, travel, venue responsibilities, any private-session arrangement and cancellation terms in writing for the specific location.

There is no universal international fee. An inquiry is an invitation to scope the work, not a booking or reserved date.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you decide.

Is this a workout or a medical assessment?

It is a guided workshop in body awareness and individual training focus. It does not diagnose injuries, treat conditions, test sporting suitability or replace medical care or your coaches' programming.

How many people and what equipment?

The useful group size depends on available space, experience and the degree of individual attention. We agree a safe number and any equipment before quoting. Participants need comfortable movement clothing.

Can it sit within an existing retreat programme?

Yes, after reviewing the schedule, audience, intensity of surrounding training, space and expected outcome. We shape the workshop so it adds clarity without overloading the guest experience.

What happens after an inquiry?

We clarify fit, participants, format, timing and responsibilities. You then receive a written scope with fee and costs. Delivery is confirmed only when both sides agree.
